Why I Am Learning to Code

I am a software engineer, and I have decided to follow one specific learning path with a clear goal getting a job in the field. This page explains my reasons and my plan.

My Reasons

I want a job where I build things

My goal is to work as a developer on real projects, with a real team, solving problems for real users. Learning to code is the most direct way to get there.

I chose focus over everything

Technology is huge, and trying to learn it all at once only creates confusion. By choosing one path, I can go deeper, build projects that prove my skills, and be ready for the roles I am applying to.

I want to grow beyond tutorials

Watching tutorials feels productive, but real growth comes from building, breaking, and fixing. I want to think like an engineer, not just a learner.

My Plan

Daily routine

Every day I follow the same simple steps:

  • Learn one new concept.
  • Practice it with code.
  • Review what I built.

Long term goals

Over the next months I will build a portfolio of real projects, practice problem solving, and prepare my CV. Every bug I fix is one step closer to my first job as a software engineer.

Conclusion

I am learning to code because I want to turn my skills into a career. The road will have mistakes and difficult days, but I am committed to finishing it.Written by a future professional developer, one line of code at a time.
